Selected Case Study • Healthy Food • Dubai, UAE

Calories Lab Growth from the ground up.

A long-term social media and performance marketing engagement that evolved from market research, content development and Meta Ads into a broader digital growth system covering social media, website traffic, Google Ads and SEO.

Social Media Specialist & Media Buyer
FEB 2024 — MAR 2026 Engagement period • with a 2-month pause
≈ AED 15K / MONTH Average social media marketing budget
HYBRID Remote execution + on-site production
01 Starting Point

Strong product. Limited digital system.

When the engagement started, Calories Lab already had strong food photography, but video content, social media marketing, website presence and the overall digital acquisition system were still at an early stage. The business relied heavily on its existing social network and organic customer relationships.

Video Content

Video production and short-form content required a stronger and more consistent system.

Paid Acquisition

A structured paid acquisition system had not yet been fully developed.

Audience Clarity

The ideal audience segments still needed to be tested and validated through campaign data.

Digital Ecosystem

Website traffic, Google Ads and SEO were not yet connected into one digital growth ecosystem.

02 My Role

More than media buying.

My role combined social media strategy, paid acquisition, content production, campaign analysis and operational coordination. The engagement was hybrid — remote for ongoing digital execution and on-site during content-production and photography sessions.

Market & Competitor Research

Studied the Dubai healthy-meals market, analyzed key competitors and developed the initial strategic direction.

Social Media Strategy

Developed the content direction, social media structure and customer communication approach.

Meta Ads Management

Planned, launched, analyzed and optimized branding, engagement and sales campaigns.

Content Production

Coordinated models, scripts, photography and video production.

Creative Supervision

Supervised the graphic design workflow and reviewed creative assets before publishing.

PR & Model Coordination

Managed communication and coordination with models used during content production.

Campaign Analysis

Analyzed campaign data and continuously refined audiences, creatives and campaign direction.

Seasonal Planning

Adjusted campaign direction according to seasonal demand and market behavior in Dubai.

Website • Google Ads • SEO

During the later stage of the project, the strategy expanded into website traffic, Google Ads and SEO.

03 Strategy

Research first. Scale second.

The first month focused on market research, competitor analysis and creating a six-month marketing plan. The initial target was approximately 60 clients within six months — averaging around 10 new clients per month.

STEP 01

Research

Market study, competitor analysis and business readiness.

STEP 02

Brand System

Content direction, scripts, production and social optimization.

STEP 03

Meta Launch

Branding, engagement and sales campaigns.

STEP 04

Optimize

Analyze data and improve audience targeting.

STEP 05

Expand

Organic reach, website, Google Ads and SEO.

04 Subscription Strategy

Lower the barrier. Let the product close the sale.

Weekly subscriptions were used as an easier entry point for new customers. Instead of asking every customer to commit immediately to a monthly plan, customers could first experience the food and service before deciding to continue with a longer subscription.
