Calories Lab Growth from the ground up.

A long-term social media and performance marketing engagement that evolved from market research, content development and Meta Ads into a broader digital growth system covering social media, website traffic, Google Ads and SEO.

01 Starting Point

Strong product. Limited digital system.

When the engagement started, Calories Lab already had strong food photography, but video content, social media marketing, website presence and the overall digital acquisition system were still at an early stage. The business relied heavily on its existing social network and organic customer relationships.

Video Content

Video production and short-form content needed a stronger and more consistent system.

Paid Acquisition

A structured paid acquisition system had not yet been fully developed.

Audience Clarity

Audience segments still needed to be tested and validated through campaign data.

02 My Role

More than media buying.

My role combined social media strategy, paid acquisition, content production, campaign analysis and operational coordination. The engagement was hybrid — remote for ongoing execution and on-site during content production and photography sessions.

Market & Competitor Research

Studied the Dubai healthy-meals market, analyzed key competitors and developed the initial strategic direction.

Social Media Strategy

Developed content direction, social media structure and customer communication approach.

Meta Ads Management

Planned, launched, analyzed and optimized branding, engagement and sales campaigns.

Content Production

Coordinated models, scripts, photography and video production.

Creative Supervision

Supervised the graphic design workflow and reviewed creative assets.

PR & Model Coordination

Managed communication and coordination with models involved in production.
